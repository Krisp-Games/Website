Corpo-Rats: Reloaded

Corpo-Rats: Reloaded is a passion project. The original came from our first University game jam; we're devoted to seeing our original vision through, with more time and gained skills throughout our journey. With the knowledge from Unreal, we're taking our next steps into the Unity engine, getting out of our comfort zone to experiment and learn. Evolving our original idea from a couch co-op split-screen experience to a fully online playable prototype and taking what we've learnt, like game management and team coordination to spearhead this project to life.
